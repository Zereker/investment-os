# Investment Lessons Learned

> Long-term lessons extracted from trading reviews and portfolio operations.

## 1. Wealth is created by holding, not by trading

The strongest contributors to portfolio growth came from holding high-conviction assets:

- MU
- TSM
- GOOG
- SPY

The system should optimize for identifying great assets and staying invested.

---

## 2. The biggest risk is leaving the circle of competence

Trading losses were mainly associated with ideas outside the core framework:

- SPCX
- MRVL
- Short-term thematic experiments

A correct industry theme does not guarantee the right company.

---

## 3. Price is a signal, not a decision

Price movement should trigger analysis, not automatic action.

Investment decisions should be driven by:

- business fundamentals
- long-term thesis
- risk/reward assessment

---

## 4. New capital should reduce concentration naturally

Do not automatically sell winners.

Use new capital allocation to gradually improve diversification:

- increase SPY/VOO exposure
- reduce single-stock concentration over time

---

## 5. Simplicity is a competitive advantage

More trades do not necessarily create more returns.

Complexity should only be added when it improves decision quality.

---

## 6. Every trade should answer one question

> If I had to hold this company for three years, why do I believe it will create value?

If the answer is unclear, the trade should be reconsidered.

---

## 7. Default action is no action

When evidence does not clearly support a trade, continue holding.

Patience is an active investment decision.
