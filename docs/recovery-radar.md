# Recovery Radar

## Purpose

Track how far the portfolio and key holdings are from previous highs. The goal is to distinguish normal volatility from thesis deterioration.

## Daily Metrics

- Portfolio drawdown from recent high
- Individual holding recovery distance
- Estimated recovery contribution
- Whether action is required

## Status Levels

### Green

Drawdown below 10%.

Normal volatility. No action unless fundamentals change.

### Yellow

Drawdown between 10% and 20%.

Review fundamentals, concentration, and risk exposure.

### Red

Drawdown above 20%.

Require deeper portfolio review before taking action.

## Operating Principle

A falling price alone is not a sell signal.

The key question is whether the investment thesis has changed.

## Example

Current portfolio drawdown is approximately 5% from recent highs.

Main pressure comes from semiconductor concentration (MU, TSM, NVDA), while other holdings remain relatively close to highs.

Decision: Monitor, no forced action.
