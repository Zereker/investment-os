# Daily Assistant Check Template

> Purpose: reduce the need to open IBKR every day. This is an assistant monitoring layer, not a change to Investment OS rules and not a trading signal system.

## Daily Check

### 1. Account Health

- Net Liquidation Value:
- Daily Change:
- Cash:
- Cash Ratio:
- Leverage:

Status:
- 🟢 Normal
- 🟡 Attention
- 🔴 Review

---

### 2. Market Movement Explanation

Question:

What mainly drove today's portfolio movement?

Report:
- Main contributors:
- Main detractors:
- Is this market noise or thesis change?

---

### 3. Position Alert

Only report abnormal events:

- Large single-day movement
- Company-specific event
- Thesis-related news

If nothing important:

> No abnormal position changes.

---

### 4. Allocation Drift Check

Check:

- Cash level
- Core ETF progress
- Alpha exposure
- Legacy holdings during transition

No action unless rules are triggered.

---

### 5. Alpha Watch

Current Alpha holdings:

- Thesis change?
- Business quality change?
- Long-term expectation change?

Price movement alone is not an action trigger.

---

### 6. Final Action

Allowed outputs only:

- NO ACTION
- WATCH
- REVIEW

Default:

> Continue executing Investment OS.

---

## Weekly Extension

Once per week add:

- Weekly return
- Largest contributor
- Largest detractor
- Drawdown status
- Constitution compliance
- Next week watch items

This template does not modify IPS, Constitution, or operating rules.
